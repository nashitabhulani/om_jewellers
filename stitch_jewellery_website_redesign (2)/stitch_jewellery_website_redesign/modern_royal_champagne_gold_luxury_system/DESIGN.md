## Brand & Style

This design system reimagines high-end bridal and heirloom jewelry commerce for modern mobile-first experiences. The visual philosophy blends royal heritage with contemporary editorial luxury. By shifting away from heavy, dark chocolate-brown backgrounds toward an airy, luminous champagne cream canvas, pieces sparkle with increased brilliance and tactile clarity. 

The aesthetic marries **Refined Luxury Minimalism** with delicate **Tactile Filigree Accents**. The interface evokes warmth, exclusivity, trust, and celebratory joy. Royal maroon is elevated to a precise, selective contrast element—reserved for high-conversion action drivers, prestige stamps, and focal badges—while warm radiant golds, delicate hair-thin keylines, and soft gradient backdrops bring effortless bridal sophistication to handheld viewports.

## Colors

The palette establishes an illuminating champagne environment anchored by historic jewelry hallmarks:

- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF7F2` & `#FDFBF7`)**: Replaces heavy dark brown expanses with warm, breathable silk cream backgrounds that let intricate diamonds, uncut polki, and 22k yellow gold breathe.
- **Primary Burgundy Accent (`#5B1318` & `#3D0C10`)**: Treated with discipline. Used exclusively for authoritative brand signatures, primary action buttons, celebratory sale callouts, and certification highlights to maintain royal weight without visual heaviness.
- **Secondary & Tertiary Radiant Gold (`#D4AF37` & `#C5A059`)**: Applied across ultra-thin card strokes, ornate botanical filigree vectors, star indicators, active navigation tabs, and micro-framing lines.
- **Gold Linear Card Gradient (`#FBF8F0` to `#F3EAD7`)**: Used subtly on hero cards and product showcases to emulate reflective metallic warmth under showroom spot lighting.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the regal character of **Playfair Display** with the clean legibility of **Plus Jakarta Sans**:

- **Editorial Serifs (`Playfair Display`)**: Governs all headlines, collection titles, bridal storytelling, and reflective section intros. Subtle italic variations (`subheading`) add an intimate, personalized boutique atmosphere to quotes and seasonal campaigns.
- **Structured Sans-Serif (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers high scannability across specifications, gold rates, live prices, filters, and checkout workflows on compact screens.
- **Eyebrow Treatments**: All uppercase micro-headers utilize extended tracking (`0.18em`) to mimic timeless luxury catalog embossing.

## Layout & Spacing

Mobile luxury interfaces require generous breathing room around precious items to reflect showroom white-glove treatment:

- **Mobile Rhythm**: Sections anchor against a 4-column layout with standard `1.25rem` outer margins and `0.75rem` to `1rem` gutters between dual product grids.
- **Vertical Air**: Section dividers and storytelling zones utilize `space-xl` and `space-2xl` pacing to avoid dense e-commerce clutter.
- **Dual vs Carousel Layouts**: Curated categories (e.g., Bangles, Polki, Solitaires) render in smooth horizontal gesture carousels with peak offsets, exposing 15% of the next card to invite discovery without overwhelming vertical viewport height.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is crafted through subtle metallic transitions, gossamer gold keylines, and ambient warm shadows:

- **Luminous Tiers**: The base background (`#FAF7F2`) transitions into cards using `#FDFBF7` with an extremely soft gradient wash (`#FBF8F0` down to `#F3EAD7`).
- **Low-Contrast Gold Keylines**: Rather than standard gray dividers, elements are structured with hairline gold borders (`1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.28)`), giving every card the aura of fine jewelry casing.
- **Ambient Warm Shadows**: Elevation uses warm amber-tinted diffused drop shadows rather than synthetic blacks:
  - *Standard Card*: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(91, 19, 24, 0.04), 0 2px 6px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.08)`
  - *Floating CTAs & Sticky Bars*: `0 10px 25px -4px rgba(61, 12, 16, 0.12), 0 2px 10px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`
- **Filigree Dividing Accents**: Section breaks feature centered dual-line hairpins with a delicate diamond-lozenge gold motif instead of stark full-width borders.

## Shapes

The design system maintains **Soft (`roundedness: 1`)** geometry, prioritizing architectural poise and classic jewelry framing over hyper-rounded informal shapes:

- **Containers & Product Cards**: Modest `0.25rem` (`4px`) to `0.5rem` (`8px`) radiuses create clean, pristine glass-cased window silhouettes.
- **Pills & Selective Badges**: Reserved solely for micro status indicators, hallmark certification tags (e.g., `BIS 916 Hallmark`, `IGI Certified`), and floating assistance buttons, which utilize full rounded capsules for ergonomic differentiation.
- **Ornamental Frames**: Selected portrait banners utilize arched header frames (`border-radius: 120px 120px 4px 4px`), evoking royal Indian palace jharokha silhouettes while maintaining modern digital discipline.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button**: Royal Maroon background (`#5B1318`), crisp off-white text (`#FDFBF7`), subtle gold border highlight on active tap. Height 48px, subtle radius (`4px`), letter-spaced sans typography (`label-button`).
- **Secondary / Luxury Outline Button**: Transparent or cream background with a 1.5px radiant gold stroke (`#C5A059`), royal maroon text (`#5B1318`), and slight inner gold gleam on interaction.
- **Direct Video / Concierge CTA**: Floating pill button with soft burgundy-to-maroon gradient, gold camera/WhatsApp micro-icon, and ambient gold shadow.

### Jewelry Product Cards
- **Structure**: Surface cream-light card (`#FDFBF7`) framed in a `1px` soft gold stroke (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.28)`).
- **Image Staging**: High-resolution image nestled against an ultra-soft radial champagne gradient highlight behind the jewelry piece.
- **Badges**: Top-left floating badge in deep royal maroon (`#5B1318`) with crisp gold text for "New Bridal Arrival" or "Bestseller".
- **Action / Wishlist**: Gold circular outline icon in top-right with soft micro-interaction scale.

### Filigree & Section Dividers
- Central ornamental geometric motif (diamond lozenge) flanked by two graduating hairlines in antique gold (`#C5A059`). Replaces standard HR tags across mobile content sections.

### Live Gold Rate Ribbon
- Pinned champagne-gold gradient ticker (`#FBF8F0` to `#F3EAD7`) below the top bar, displaying 22K and 24K gold rates per gram in crisp maroon font with subtle gold sparkle icons.

### Form Inputs & Inquiry Modals
- Inputs set against `#FFFFFF` or `#FDFBF7` with bottom-only or thin 1px ambient champagne borders (`#D4AF37` at 35% opacity), transitioning to focused royal maroon accents. Clear typography in `14px` Plus Jakarta Sans to facilitate quick bridal lounge appointments.

### Filter & Category Chips
- Minimalist rectangular chips with rounded corners (`4px`), champagne fill, and hairline gold edges. Active state turns royal maroon fill with gleaming gold lettering.
